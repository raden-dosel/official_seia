How the Process Works

A clear, step-by-step approach from your initial inquiry to long-term system reliability.

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Contact us via our online form or direct phone line with your security requirements.

  2. 2

    Site Assessment

    Our technicians evaluate perimeter dimensions, electrical layout, and access points.

  3. 3

    Recommendation

    Receive a tailored layout proposal and itemized quotation matching your property specifications.

  4. 4

    Installation

    Certified technicians securely mount brackets, run wiring, energizers, or gate motors.

  5. 5

    Testing & Handover

    Comprehensive voltage/limit testing, fail-safe checks, and client operation walkthrough.

  6. 6

    Maintenance & Support

    Routine check-ups, system recalibration, and fast-response repair support.
